Privacy Policy – RegenApp

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Controller: mammutandroid (private individual)
Contact: mammutandroid@gmail.com

What data does the app process?

RegenApp works without a user account, shows no ads, embeds no analytics tools and stores no personal data on third-party servers.

Stored on the device (and only there):

  • the places you have saved, with their coordinates,
  • the place last shown and the selected forecast model,
  • a cache for map tiles and weather data.

This data does not leave the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Location: When you tap "Use current location", the app requests permission for your approximate location. The position is used solely to retrieve the weather for your area and is not stored permanently. You can use the app fully without this permission and choose places via the search instead.

Who is data transmitted to?

In order to display the forecast and the map, the app retrieves data from the following services. In doing so, the coordinates of the displayed place or map section and – for technical reasons – your device's IP address are transmitted:

No identifiers are transmitted that would identify you personally – no account, no advertising ID, no device identifier. The providers listed process this data under their own privacy policies.

Legal basis

Processing of the location data is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the system settings. Retrieval of the weather and map data serves the purpose of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the app stores no personal data on the controller's servers, access and erasure concern only the data held locally on your device; you remove this by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
